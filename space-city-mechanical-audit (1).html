Pillar 2 (11/25) — Website Conversion. You already have two things most HVAC sites your size skip: a working Financing page and a live booking link through Housecall Pro. Credit where it's due. But there's no sticky WhatsApp/Email bar following the reader down the page, no dedicated emergency/24‑hour landing page (even though your own footer advertises 24‑hour emergency calls), and — this is the big one — PageSpeed Insights clocked your mobile Largest Contentful Paint at 24.9 seconds, with a Performance score of 29/100. Your SEO score is a genuinely excellent 100/100 — the bones are good. The page is just too heavy to load fast enough for someone standing in a hot kitchen looking at their phone.
Pillar 3 (15/25) — Review Authority. A 4.9 average across 69 reviews, several customers on record for 10, 13, even 15+ years — that's the kind of loyalty most HVAC companies can't buy. This isn't a criticism — it means every one of your 69 five‑star reviews and twenty years of reputation was earned with zero paid visibility. It also means this is the single most wide-open pillar to fix, because there's no waste to clean up first — just a blank canvas.

Glossary of Terms

BI Score — Business Intelligence Score, a 0–100 rating of your online visibility across four pillars.
CPA — Cost Per Acquisition, what one paying customer costs to win.
CPC — Cost Per Click, what you pay each time someone clicks an ad.
CPL — Cost Per Lead, what one inquiry costs before it becomes a customer.
CTR — Click-Through Rate, the share of people who see a listing and click it.
Citation — A directory listing of your business name, address, and phone number.
EPA 608 — The federal certification required to legally handle refrigerant.
GA4 / GTM — Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager, the tools that track what happens after someone visits your site.
GBP — Google Business Profile, your free listing on Google Search and Maps.
Heat Pump — A system that both heats and cools a home by moving heat rather than generating it.
IAQ — Indoor Air Quality, filtration and ventilation services.
LTV — Lifetime Value, total revenue a customer generates over the years you keep them.
Local Services Ads / LSA — Google's pay-per-lead ad format that appears above everything else in search.
Map Pack — The three local business listings Google shows at the top of a local search.
Maintenance Agreement — A recurring service contract for seasonal tune-ups.
NAP — Name, Address, Phone — must match exactly everywhere your business is listed online.
NATE Certification — North American Technician Excellence, the industry's leading technician credential.
Refrigerant Leak Rate — How quickly a system loses refrigerant, a key diagnostic and compliance metric.
ROAS — Return on Ad Spend, revenue generated per dollar spent on ads.
RSA — Responsive Search Ad, Google's standard text-ad format.
Review Velocity — How many new reviews you earn per month.
Schema Markup — Structured code on your website that helps Google (and AI tools) understand your content.
SEER / SEER2 — The efficiency rating of an AC or heat pump system.
Thermostat (Smart vs. Standard) — Wi-Fi-connected, app-controlled thermostats vs. manual dial/digital units.
Financing / 0% APR Plan — Payment plans that let customers spread the cost of a replacement over time.
